PastryNeonTheme

Pilot first

A Marshmallow theme taken from the Pastry Marketing brand: near-black navy, electric lime and hot pink, plus the cyan and violet we carry over from Account Boosters, Pastry's previous name. Heavy condensed headlines, halftone dots and light rays. It replaces the generic blue and purple look, and works in dark and light mode.

01 · Heritage

From Account Boosters to Pastry

The announcement poster keeps Account Boosters' cyan rocket and adds Pastry's striped slice. We keep both, so the CRM still feels familiar to people who knew the old brand.

The slice stripe (from the logo)

Used as a thin 4–5px band only: under the top bar, on the login art and in the sidebar logo. Never as a fill behind text.

What each colour means in the CRM

  • Lime · action. Main buttons, active nav, "do this".
  • Pink · attention. Urgent, focus rings, counts.
  • Booster cyan · info. Links, Quo, schedules, neutral progress.
  • Booster violet · AI. AI status and automation only.
  • Sun · warning. Needs action, pending approval.
  • Cherry · critical. Errors and critical alerts only.

06 · Light mode rules

Same brand on white

Light mode is a full theme, not an afterthought. The rules that keep it readable:

What flips

  • Buttons go navy with lime text. Lime is never text on white (1.2:1).
  • Pink deepens to #C0106A for links, focus and labels.
  • Cyan and violet deepen to #0E7490 and #6D28D9 when used as text.

What stays

  • Login art and page hero stay dark, like the poster, in both modes.
  • Bright colours stay as fills: dots, bars, stripe and status edges.
  • Meanings never change between modes, and every status has a label.
07 · Rollout

Pilot, review, then everywhere

The pilot is switched on per person, so it can be tried on live work without changing anything for the rest of the team.

1

Approve this plan

Sir signs off on colours, font and the details above, or asks for changes.

2

Build the pilot

  • A "Pastry theme (pilot)" switch in the user menu
  • Applies to: Login, Leads (urgent table and cards), Lead notes, Processor Board, Notifications, Sidebar
  • Colours come from shared theme tokens with dark and light values, so nothing is repainted by hand
3

Try it for a week

Pilot users work in it day to day, in both modes and on phones. We collect what reads badly or feels too loud and adjust.

4

Roll out to all

  • Make it the default for every page and role
  • Remove the switch
  • Match Donut (the extension) in a separate release
08 · Decisions needed

Four questions for Sir

1. Headline fontUse Anton for titles and big numbers? It is a free Google font and adds one small font download. Recommended: yes. The alternative is Inter Extra Bold everywhere, which is safer but less like the poster.
2. Account Boosters coloursBring the rocket cyan and violet into the CRM for info and AI? Recommended: yes, it ties the new brand to the old one and gives info and AI their own colour instead of reusing pink.
3. Who gets the pilotAdmins only, or anyone who switches it on? Recommended: Admins and CS Admins first, then everyone during week 2.
4. Default lookShould the CRM open in dark mode by default once rolled out? The brand is dark-first. Recommended: follow the device setting, dark when unsure, light mode one click away.
