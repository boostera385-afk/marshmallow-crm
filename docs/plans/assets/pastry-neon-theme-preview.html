Marshmallow CRM · theme proposal for approval

PastryNeonTheme

Pilot first

A Marshmallow theme taken from the Pastry Marketing poster style: near-black navy, electric lime, hot pink, heavy condensed headlines, halftone dots and light rays. It replaces the generic blue and purple look. The plan is to pilot it on the screens we already updated, then roll it out to the whole CRM once Sir approves.

Status: plan only, nothing built yetPilot: 6 screensDark-first, with a light mode
01 · Colour

Palette from the poster

Lime and pink are the two brand voices. Lime means "go / do this", pink means "look here / urgent". Status colours (red, amber, green) keep their usual meaning so nobody has to relearn what a warning looks like.

03 · Signature details

Four poster moves, used sparingly

These keep it from looking like a stock template. Each one has a fixed place so the CRM stays calm to work in.

Urgent

Pink sticker. Slightly tilted pink label, only for Urgent Job and the urgent count.

Notifications

Halftone dots in empty corners of the login and page headers. Never behind text.

Light rays on the login panel only, from the poster's cake-slice glow.

06 · Rollout

Pilot, review, then everywhere

The pilot is switched on per person, so it can be tried on live work without changing anything for the rest of the team.

1

Approve this plan

Sir signs off on colours, font and the details above, or asks for changes.

2

Build the pilot

  • A "Pastry theme (pilot)" switch in the user menu
  • Applies to: Login, Leads (urgent table and cards), Lead notes, Processor Board, Notifications, Sidebar
  • Colours come from shared theme tokens, so nothing is repainted by hand
3

Try it for a week

Pilot users work in it day to day. We collect what reads badly or feels too loud and adjust.

4

Roll out to all

  • Make it the default for every page and role
  • Remove the switch
  • Match Donut (the extension) in a separate release
07 · Decisions needed

Three questions for Sir

1. Headline fontUse Anton for titles and big numbers? It is a free Google font and adds one small font download. Recommended: yes. The alternative is Inter Extra Bold everywhere, which is safer but less like the poster.
2. Who gets the pilotAdmins only, or anyone who switches it on? Recommended: Admins and CS Admins first, then everyone during week 2.
3. Default lookShould the CRM open in dark mode by default once rolled out? The brand is dark-first. Recommended: dark by default, light mode still one click away.
